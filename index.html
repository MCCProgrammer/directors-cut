Director’s Cut for Letterboxd

The extended edition of Letterboxd: the features you wish the site had, built right into the pages you already use.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store Privacy policy

🎲 Watchlist Roulette

Can’t decide what to watch? Spin your watchlist, any list, or the films you and a friend both want to see.

🎬 Film details

Rating, views, runtime and synopsis in one window. Add to your watchlist, like or rate without leaving the page.

📺 Where to stream

Where a film is streaming in your country, powered by JustWatch, in 40+ countries.

👥 Friends’ ratings

Which of the people you follow watched the film, with their stars, and who has it on their watchlist.

💚 Fav Match

Find the members who share the most favorite films with any profile.

⭐ Keep your friends close

Your friends’ profiles and favorite pages pinned to the top of every Letterboxd page.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads and no developer server. Your settings stay in your browser, and the extension never sees your Letterboxd password. Read the full privacy policy.
